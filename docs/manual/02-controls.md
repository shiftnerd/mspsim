# CONTROLS

*Every key, every button, and what each one does when you are sitting down.*

---

## THE BASICS

You walk along the floor of a side-on office. Doors, machines, chairs and coworkers each have a little arrow that floats above them
when you are close enough to use them. **Stand next to something, see the arrow, press `E`.** That is about eighty percent of the
game.

> **TIP:** You can see these keys in-game at any time: press `Esc`, choose **CONTROLS** and turn the pages with `Left` / `Right`.

---

## KEYBOARD: STANDING UP

### Moving and using

| ACTION | KEYS | NOTES |
|---|---|---|
| **Walk** | `A` `D` or `Left` `Right` | |
| **Run** | Hold `Shift` | While walking. |
| **Jump** | `Space`, `W` or `Up` | You crouch for a moment, then leap. It is a very office-sized jump. |
| **Duck** | `S` or `Down` (hold) | You stop while ducking. Handy during a nerf fight. |
| **Use, sit, open doors** | `E` | Works on whatever has the arrow over it. |
| **Pick a target** | `Tab` (next), `Shift`+`Tab` (previous) | Moves the arrow to the next thing within reach without walking. See below. |
| **Cell phone** | `P` | Press `P` or `Esc` again to put it away. |
| **Pause, help** | `Esc` or `Pause` | Opens the pause menu. |
| **Mute or restore the sound** | `M` | |
| **Change the picture shape** | `F9` | Cycles `wide`, `h`, `v`, `vclose`. |
| **Hide or show the clock and counters** | `F10` | Handy for filming. |

### People and gestures

| ACTION | KEYS | NOTES |
|---|---|---|
| **Talk** | `E` next to a coworker | |
| **Ask a coworker to follow you** | `C` next to them | Press `C` again to send them back to their day. Somebody who is not speaking to you will shake their head. |
| **Wave** | `Q` | |
| **Sip your drink** | `F` | Only while you are holding one. A cup lasts a few sips, longer when you are sitting. |
| **Dance** | `X` | Press `X` again, move, jump or press `E` to stop. |

> **NOTE:** You pick up a coffee at the machine, a water at the cooler and a drink at the fridge by pressing `E` next to them.
> You then walk around holding it, like a professional.

### The nerf blaster

Take a blaster from the rack in the cubicle area (`E`). It holds a small handful of darts. Press `E` at the rack again to reload.

| ACTION | KEYS | NOTES |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Tap `Z` | A quick dart. |
| **Long shot** | Hold `Z`, then let go | A dotted arc shows where the dart will land. The counter reads `AIMING n%`. Full power takes a bit over a second. |
| **Put it away** | `G` | |
| **Offer a nerf war** | `V` (while holding the blaster) | Ask the room if it wants to. |
| **Pick up a dart** | Walk over it | Darts that miss end up on the floor. |

> **TIP:** Ducking (`S`) while somebody fires at you is exactly as useful as it looks.

> **NOTE:** Darts stick in walls, monitors and furniture. Monitors flicker when hit. Coworkers react in character. Some laugh. One
> files a complaint with his eyebrows.

## KEYBOARD: WHILE SEATED

Walk to a chair and press **`E`** to sit. Most of the keys change meaning while you are sitting down.

| ACTION | KEYS | NOTES |
|---|---|---|
| **Open the computer** | `E` | At a desk with a computer. |
| **Answer a ringing phone** | `E` | At a desk with a ringing phone. It takes priority over the computer. |
| **Roll your chair** | Hold `Shift` and press `A` / `D` (or the arrows) | The chair speeds up, coasts and slows by itself, stops at walls and desks, and may bump somebody. |
| **Sip your drink** | `F` | Your cup stands on the desk when you sit. |
| **Stand up** | Any move key (`A`, `D`, arrows) or `Space` | At a seat with no computer (the conference room, the couch), `E` also stands you up. |
| **Phone, pause, mute** | `P`, `Esc`, `M` | As usual. |

> **NOTE:** At a desk with a computer, `E` opens the computer. Press a move key to stand up. You will not be trapped in the chair: a
> plain move key always works.

> **TIP:** While you are seated, you will stretch, scratch, yawn and look about on your own. This is called "authentic."

## THE `TAB` SELECTION

Sometimes two things are within reach: a door and a coworker, a monitor and a rubber duck, a row of network ports. `E` normally
picks the closest one. **`Tab`** moves the arrow to the next thing in reach, left to right. **`Shift`+`Tab`** goes back. Walk away
about ten pixels and the game goes back to choosing for you.

## GAMEPAD

Plug it in and go. A gamepad covers walking, using things and the menus. For anything it does not cover, there is the keyboard.

### In the office

| ACTION | BUTTON | NOTES |
|---|---|---|
| **Walk** | Left stick, or D-pad left / right | |
| **Run** | Hold `B` | |
| **Jump** | `A`, or D-pad up | |
| **Duck** | D-pad down, or stick down | |
| **Use / sit / open** | `X` | The same as `E`. |
| **Wave** | `Y` | |
| **Sip your drink** | `LB` | |
| **Cell phone** | `Back` / `Select` | |
| **Pause** | `Start` | |
| **Roll your chair** (seated) | Hold `B` and push the stick or D-pad sideways | The same as `Shift` + `A` / `D`. |
| **Stand up** (seated) | Move, or press `A` | |

### In menus, cards and screens

| ACTION | BUTTON |
|---|---|
| **Move the highlight** | D-pad or left stick |
| **OK** | `A` |
| **Back** | `B` |

### Not on the pad (yet)

These are keyboard-only today: **dance**, **follow**, **mute**, **the outfit swap**, **Tab**, and **aiming and firing the nerf blaster**. The pad has `RT` and `RB` marked for aiming and picking targets in the key map, but the player does
not listen to them yet.

> **NOTE (COMING SOON):** The way the game reads its controls is being reworked so that more than one controller can be used at
> once. When that lands, this table is going to grow.

## KEYS INSIDE THE LITTLE SCREENS

Most things that take over the screen read the same few keys. When in doubt: **arrows move, `Enter` chooses, `Esc` backs out.**

| SCREEN | KEYS |
|---|---|
| **Your computer** | Mouse for everything. `Up` / `Down` move in lists, `Enter` opens, `Esc` steps back one level, then closes. Details in [Chapter 5](05-your-computer-and-phone.md). |
| **Your phone** | Arrows move, `Enter` opens, `Esc` goes back, `P` closes. Mouse works. |
| **A decision card** | `Up` / `Down` then `Enter`, or press `1` to `4` to choose that option straight away, or click. `Esc` leaves where it is allowed. A card ignores you for a split second when it appears, so you do not choose by accident. |
| **The end-of-day report** | `Enter` or `Space` to continue. |
| **The title and pause menus** | Arrows and `Enter`. See [Chapter 1](01-getting-started.md). |
| **Outings** | Each game has its own keys, listed in [Chapter 6](06-after-work.md). |

## FREE PLAY EXTRAS

These only work in **Free Play** (not during a campaign):

| KEY | WHAT IT DOES |
|---|---|
| `1` to `7` | Swap to one of the stock people: intern, adult, Dan, Alan, Alan on site, Garrison, John. |
| `Y` | Change your outfit (the creator opens over the room). |
| `O` | Take the team out ([Chapter 6](06-after-work.md)). |
| `` ` `` (backtick) | Cycle a "twist" in the world, such as a themed week. The HUD tags it with a `*`. |
| `H`, `K`, `R` | Hurt, fall over and get back up. For filming. |
| `Shift`+`F10` | Open the toolbox ([Chapter 8](08-tips-and-troubleshooting.md#the-free-play-toolbox)). |

## THE HUD KEYS

| KEY | WHAT IT DOES |
|---|---|
