# GAME MODES

*Six doors on the title menu. All of them lead to the same office, which has never once been tidy.*

---

## AT A GLANCE

| MODE | WHO YOU ARE | HOW LONG | SAVES | DAY OR WEEK |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tech Campaign** | A service desk tech | 20 working days | 3 slots | `DAY n/20` |
| **Owner Campaign** | One of the company's owners | 20 weeks | 3 slots | `WEEK n/20` |
| **Service Manager** | The person who runs the desk | 20 working days | 3 slots | `DAY n/20` |
| **Sales** | The account manager | 20 weeks | 3 slots | `WEEK n/20` |
| **Free Play / Film Mode** | Anyone you like | As long as you like | none | none |
| **Career** | Yourself, across every run | Forever | automatic | none |

> **NOTE:** This chapter tells you **what each mode is about**. It does not tell you what happens. The office likes to keep its
> surprises for the people who turn up.

---

## TECH CAMPAIGN

The main game, and the one to try first. You are a tech on the service desk of Pixel IT Partners, a small managed service provider.
Work arrives as tickets: you find the right machine in the remote management tool, decide how to fix it, and close the ticket in the
service board. Around the tickets there are coworkers with moods and memories, clients who phone, an inbox that fills, and an office that
rewards being decent to people. Jobs, outages and surprises happen. Your energy matters, your reputation matters, and so does the
coffee machine.

*You will use:* the [computer and phone](05-your-computer-and-phone.md), the [whole building](04-the-office.md), and the [after-work
outings](06-after-work.md).

> **NOTE:** Do well and the company sends you to a trade conference for a day (an owner can buy a ticket too). You walk the
> resort. Each door in the hall is a session: the card asks whether you want a seat, then **sit on a free chair (`E`)** and the talk
> plays line by line. **`E` or `Enter`** skips to the next line, and you stand up when it is over. The expo hall is open all day.

## OWNER CAMPAIGN

Same company, same building, much worse sleep. You play one of the two owners, and the question changes from "how do I fix this?"
to "how do we afford this?" Prices, hiring, tools, clients, collections and the mood of the team all land on your desk, and there
is a partner who has opinions of their own. The week is the unit of time, and cash is on the HUD where tickets used to be.

## SERVICE MANAGER

You have been promoted from working the desk to running it. The big question is no longer "how do I fix this?" but **who** should, and when.
Work arrives on a dispatch board that you sort, assign, escalate and watch fill, with a runbook library to keep up to date and a
team whose capacity and morale you can read and ignore at your peril.

## SALES

The company's account manager, with a bell on the wall and a quota to hit. You find prospects, quote jobs and close deals, and
then the rest of the team has to deliver what you promised. A customer relationship tool replaces the ticket board on your
desktop, and the HUD shows the week, the quarter, your progress to quota and how many working hours you have left.

## FREE PLAY / FILM MODE

The office as a sandbox. No scoring, no saves, no consequences. Wander, sit anywhere, swap outfits (`Y`), take the team out
(`O`), start a nerf war, or open the **toolbox** (`Shift`+`F10`) to play any one of the small games with settings of your
choice. Press `F10` to hide the clock and counters and film in peace. See [Chapter 8](08-tips-and-troubleshooting.md#the-free-play-toolbox).

## CAREER

Not a mode so much as a trophy shelf. Career keeps your **lifetime counters** (pages of them) and a **medal board**, each medal
with a hint of how to earn it. Some medals unlock things you can use: a wallpaper for your office computer, a ringtone for your
phone, clothes and accessories in the character creator. Open it from the title menu, or from the phone's **SETTINGS** app.

| KEY | WHAT IT DOES |
|---|---|
| `Left` / `Right` (or `A` / `D`) | Turn the page. |
| `C` | Jump to the **collection** pages: the cast you have met and your level with each, karaoke songs sung, escape room clue types seen, printers beaten, and this run's office decor. |
| `F9` | Change the picture shape. |
| `Esc` or `Backspace` | Back out. |

---

## YEAR 2

Finish a Tech or Owner campaign and its submenu on the title screen gains a **START YEAR 2** row.

Year 2 is a harder second pass at the same company. Some of what you learned comes with you (a little of your skill, your
standing, a few extra points to spend on your build), the clocks run tighter, the world throws more twists, and the calendar
has been shuffled. The character screen gains a **MUT** chip: press `M` (or `Shift`+`M`) to cycle the twist, and `B` to flip between
**PICK** (you choose the twist) and **BAN** (you choose one to avoid).

> **TIP:** Year 2 is not a victory lap. If your first year was cozy, you may want to leave the level on Easy.

---

## CHOOSING A MODE: A QUICK GUIDE

| IF YOU... | TRY |
|---|---|
| Have never played | **Tech Campaign**, on Easy |
| Like spreadsheets and consequences | **Owner Campaign** |
| Like queues and juggling | **Service Manager** |
| Like talking people into things | **Sales** |
| Just want to look around | **Free Play** |
| Want to see what you have unlocked | **Career** |
