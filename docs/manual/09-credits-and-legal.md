- Defects in the software that make it do something the manual says it does not do.

## CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Something not working? Something unclear in this manual? Something in 2014 that should not be?

**Open an issue** on the project's page. Please include:

| WHAT | EXAMPLE |
|---|---|
| **What you did** | "I pressed `E` at the warehouse door with a cup of coffee." |
| **What you expected** | "To go through the door." |
| **What happened** | "Nothing. The arrow went grey." |
| **Your Godot version** | "4.7.2" |
| **Your picture shape** | "wide" (the pause menu shows it in the **SHAPE** row) |
| **A screenshot** | If you can. |

> **NOTE:** MSPSim is a private project, so the issue tracker is visible only to the people who can see the project. If you cannot see
> it, you are not on the list. This is working as intended.

> **TIP:** Before you ask, check [Chapter 8: Tips and Troubleshooting](08-tips-and-troubleshooting.md). It has most of the answers.
> The ones it does not have are the interesting ones.
