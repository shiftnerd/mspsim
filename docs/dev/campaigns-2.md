### Reviewable story: the game bible and the editable text file
- **`docs/GAME_BIBLE.md`**: a hand-written, readable game guide / pitch bible for reviewers (pitch, tone and the 2013-14 rules, cast, core loop, the 20-day tech arc as a table, the big jobs, the owner campaign, things to poke, review questions, what is not built). Keep it up to date when the design changes.
- **`docs/manual/`**: the player-facing, spoiler-free instruction manual (game-console style): getting started, controls, modes, the office, computer and phone, outings, the creator, tips; screenshots in `docs/manual/img/`. Update it when controls, menus or screens change.
- **`godot/story/STORY.md`**: generated, read-only, line-by-line story in play order (day by day, the big jobs with all choices, the owner weeks, dialogue, tickets, endings), each text ending with its `[key]`.
- **`godot/story/strings.txt`**: every story string (579 of them) from the data tables as plain text under `[key]` lines; editing it changes the game. The `Strings` autoload (`strings.gd`, last in the list) reads it at startup, replaces the text in the live tables, ignores edits that change `%s` / `%d` markers, reports unknown keys, empty text and 2013-14 slips as warnings, and keeps the code's text as the default (`DEFAULT_<NAME>` constants).
  To make that possible the generator turns the story tables into `const DEFAULT_X` plus a mutable `var X = DEFAULT_X.duplicate(true)` (`PATCHABLE` and `make_patchable` in `tools/make_godot.py`; those scripts carry `# gdlint:disable=class-definitions-order,class-variable-name`). Covered: `Story.DAYS`, `Playbook.BOOKS/PROBLEMS`, `Owner.CARDS`, `Msp.PROBLEMS/RANSOM/ADOBE/INSTALL`, `Talk` lines, `Nerds.THREADS/JOKES`, `Lync.MESSAGES`, `Review.QUOTES/EPILOGUES`, `Social.THANKS`, `Shift` texts, `Hints.DAY1`, `Growth.STRETCH`.
  **Still inside the code** (not in the file): job outcome emails and follow-up tickets (`Playbook._outcome`), the day 9/11/14/20 callbacks, all-hands speeches, owner cards built from the numbers, growth-plan prompts. (An attempt to move those out automatically was dropped as too risky; `INLINE = {}` in the generator is the hook.)
- **Tools** (`tests/story_export.gd -- --init|--sync|--doc|--check`, see `godot/story/README.md`): `--sync` after code changes keeps reviewers' edits and adds new strings; `--doc` regenerates `STORY.md`; `--check` lists problems. Test: `tests/strings.gd` (the committed file matches the code, edits apply, bad edits are rejected, sync keeps edits).

### Client profitability, projects and services (`scripts/projects.gd` autoload `Projects`, owner campaign)
- **Hours per client**: `Projects.hours()` turns tech-seconds into hours (15 s = 1 h); a fee includes `fee/66` hours (`included_hours`). `record_week()` (from `Owner.settle`) keeps the last weeks (`hours_hist`); `avg_hours`, `over_hours` show who costs more than they pay. Boardroom CLIENTS has an HOURS column (`+n` over), the profit review card quotes the hours.
- **Profit review answers**: RAISE FEE 20%, ENFORCE THE SCOPE, BILL A CHANGE ORDER (`change_order`: four weeks of over-hours x $95, 60% accept at mood 60+, else 35%, sets `scope_fixed`), OTHER -> `profit_fire` card: FIRE POLITELY (30 days notice), FIRE NOW, KEEP. `fire_client` rolls **bad word of mouth** (unhappy and sudden firings are worst): `wom_weeks` 6, rep -2, a prospect card, Vic closes less (`vic_penalty`). Happy polite exits can bring a referral lead.
- **Services = recurring revenue** (`SERVICES`: backup $110/wk cost $35, patching $80/$25): sold by cards (`_service_card`; backup needs the $600 platform + $100/wk first, flag `backup_service`). They add to `weekly_estimate`, keep backups healthy and cut patch debt.
- **Projects = one-time money** (`TYPES`: desktops, network, server, backup_dr, security): Vic brings a quote card with four methods (fixed price / scope first with a $300 discovery and no creep / time and materials / pass). Fixed jobs creep 30% of the time (change order / hold scope / eat it). Running projects take team capacity (`drag()`, max 35%). Completion banks revenue - hardware (`_complete`), reputation, and backup_dr chains a backup service sale.
- Project income is **left out of the valuation** (`take_week_income()` is subtracted from the history EBITDA); the weekly report has a RECURRING + PROJECTS row; Boardroom has a PROJECTS tab. Saved in the campaign (`projects` section). `practice_scope` counts as a maturity practice.

### Ducking (nerf + everyone) (`player.gd`, `npc.gd`, `nerf.gd`)

### Difficulty and seeds (`scripts/difficulty.gd` autoload `Difficulty`, roadmap item 1 of `docs/replayability-ideas.md`)
- **What it is**: a tier (`easy` / `normal` / `hard`, default normal), a run seed and one seeded random stream per system. `Difficulty.f("karaoke.gap")` (and `i()`) reads a number from `TABLE` (`key: [easy, normal, hard]`, the ONLY place the numbers live; `at(key, tier)` for tests). Normal is exactly the old constants (`tests/difficulty.gd` asserts it). Keys: `karaoke.gap / perfect / good`, `bowl.opp_skill / aim_speed / power_speed`, `escape.time / wrong_penalty`, `dinner.reveal` (faces shown), `printer.speed / zone / questions`, `nerf.gap` (x the coworkers' shot interval). Energy, family and health are never inputs (cozy Easy softens the energy effects on purpose, see the last section); a harder tier only changes numbers, keeps every perfect run reachable (a test checks notes stay apart, windows nested, 3 escape stars after two slips) and adds no content.
- **Randomness**: `Difficulty.rng("stream")` is one persistent `RandomNumberGenerator` per stream name (`karaoke`, `bowl`, `escape`, `dinner`, `printer`, `nerf`, later `events`), seeded from the run seed; `rng_for(stream, salt)` makes an independent one (a day, a setup); `shuffle(list, stream)` / `pick(list, stream)` replace `Array.shuffle()` / `randi()`. `seed_run(n)` fixes the seed (tests, a future daily seed) and restarts every stream; `new_run()` (called by `Campaign.new_game`) rolls a fresh random 24-bit seed unless `seed_run` pinned one; `seed_text()` is the 6-digit hex shown as "RUN 4F2A9C". The mini-games (`Outings`, `PrinterGame`, `Nerf`) no longer call the global `randf/randi/shuffle`; `Msp`, `Sales`, `World` (meeting gap, work legs, plan kinds) and `Onsite` (job spawn / pick, trip time, event rolls) use streams since R10a; `Owner` and the cosmetic toys still use the global generator.
- **Title menu**: the character select screen (every new run, tech / owner / Free Play, both steps) has a `LEVEL < NORMAL >` row on its OPTIONS page (R35: it was a chip on every page, and Up / Down changed it; now Up / Down pick the row and **Left / Right** or a click on an arrow change it, so the difficulty is set before the build); the choice is saved in `user://mspsim_settings.cfg` (`[difficulty] tier`, `Difficulty.settings_path`; the title loads it in `_ready`, scenes launched directly by tests stay on normal). The summary line and the start toast end with the tier ("TECH CAMPAIGN - ADULT - IFONE 5 - NORMAL"; the toast adds "RUN <seed>" when the screen is 300 px wide or more). Continue skips the screen and uses the saved tier.
- **Saves**: campaign saves carry a `difficulty` section (`{version, tier, seed}`); `continue_game` restores both; a save without it loads as normal with a fresh seed (so old saves are unchanged). Since R10a the position of every stream is saved too (see "Seeds, daily seed and the Free Play toolbox"); an old save without it restarts its streams from the seed. Free Play is not saved and uses the chosen tier.
- **In the games**: karaoke chart spacing and hit windows are stored in `g.gap / perfect_win / good_win` (`_song_secs()` replaces the three copies of `1.5 + 0.75 * SONG_LEN`; the 45 px/s scroll is unchanged); bowling aim / power speed and an opponent skill offset; escape clock and the in-room wrong-code penalty; dinner hides one face per round on hard (the points still count, but you learn nothing about that person: `g.hidden`, "?" bubble, "??" face); printer marker speed, green / yellow zone width (x `printer.zone`) and 1 / 2 / 3 test-page questions; nerf war shot interval.
- Tests: `tests/difficulty.gd` (streams, table, settings file, same seed = same charts / codes / diners / printer / bowling, each tier in each game, save / load / old save), `tests/difficulty_shots.gd` (renderer, `-- --format=wide|h|v`, `user://difficulty_FORMAT_STEP.png`), plus tier checks in `tests/charselect.gd`. A `level()` that mixes progress and skill is not built (the tier alone is used).
- **Seed-pinned tests**: a test whose outcome depends on a draw (dinner moods, pitches, findings, rival skill) calls `Difficulty.seed_run(n)` right after `new_game` (and again before each dependent section), so the order of the dependent outings is the same on every run: `tests/inroom.gd` (4240 / 4241 before the dinner, 4242 / 4244 before cornhole and the cookout), `tests/outings.gd` (4240, 4241 before the dinner) and `tests/visits.gd` (4240; the ticket, project and note counts are `>=`, the rest of the world may add its own).

### Mastery ramp and moving sweet spots (`difficulty.gd`, `career.gd`, `outings.gd`; tests `tests/mastery.gd`, `tests/zones.gd`)
- **Mastery (per game, not per day)**: every GOOD play of a game (2+ stars, finished; Esc / leaving early never counts) adds one to the global Career counter `good.<game>` (`Career.note_good`, called from `note_outing` for bowling / karaoke / escape / dinner / cornhole / grill / chair and from `note_printer` for the printer game; counters live in the career file, so they cross runs and slots). `Difficulty.mastery(game)` = clamp(good / `RAMP_PLAYS` (10), 0, 1); `0` when `Difficulty.fixed_take` is set (Free Play toolbox takes stay repeatable) and on a fresh career, so a first play, and a day 17 first play, uses exactly the TABLE numbers.
- **The blend**: `Difficulty.f(key)` is `blend(key, tier, mastery(game_of(key)))` for the keys in `MASTERY_KEYS` (game from the key prefix: karaoke, bowl -> bowling, escape, dinner, printer, grill, chair, corn -> cornhole); `at(key, tier)` is still the raw table. `blend` moves the tier's value toward a target by `m`: normal -> the HARD value; hard -> hard + `HARD_OVER` (0.25) x (hard - normal), the fairness cap; easy (cozy included) -> the NORMAL value but only `EASY_RAMP` (0.35) of the way at full mastery. `tests/mastery.gd` checks monotonic, the cap, easy < normal, m 0 = table, fixed_take = 0, karaoke windows still < gap / 2 at full mastery.
- **Scale**: karaoke.gap / perfect / good; bowl.opp_skill / aim_speed / power_speed / oil / spin_noise; escape.time / wrong_penalty; printer.speed / zone; grill.cook_rate / window / time; chair.crash / stamina / slick / par / skill; corn.opp_skill / aim_speed / power_speed. **Fixed on purpose**: whole numbers and switches (karaoke.grace / grace_drain, escape.digits / dial, dinner.reveal, printer.questions / chain, corn.to / assist, grill.tickets), random frequencies (grill.flare / dogs), the nerf war and campaign dials, and all prizes, costs and star thresholds. Dinner counts good plays but has nothing continuous to ramp (its only key is the integer `dinner.reveal`).
- **UI**: after a good play the outing result page adds one line, `MASTERY n/10. SHARPER NEXT TIME.` (`FULL MASTERY.` at 10; none for dinner, 1 star, early exits or Free Play toolbox takes).
- **Moving sweet spots**: the green zone's LOCATION changes per attempt, its width does not. `Difficulty.zone(game, lo, hi)` draws from its own stream `"<game>_zone"` (so the existing `bowl`, `grill`, `cornhole` draws and the seeded tests keep their sequences; the streams are saved with the run, a daily seed repeats them and `take_reset` restarts them in fixed takes). Bowling: `g.shift` (+-0.12 on the aim bar, through `BowlVariety.center/accuracy(..., shift)`) and `g.pcen` (power best point 0.62 to 0.90) rolled by `_bowl_zone_roll()` when a roll starts; the HUD draws both greens where they are. Cornhole: `aim_c` / `pow_c` (0.32 to 0.68 / 0.42 to 0.78) rolled by `roll_zone()` each of your bags when the game gets a `zone_rng` param (the strip draws the green there; without it the classic 0.5 / 0.6). Grill: each new patty gets `woff` (+-0.07 on the cook gauge, clamped clear of the burn line) used by `flip_range(kind, off)`. The printer pull zone already moved (`_new_zone`, 0.22 to 0.78); karaoke notes already pick a random lane per note; escape codes and the chair race props are seeded random per run, and the chair's push rhythm is a timing, not a place, so it stays. `tests/zones.gd` checks movement, bounds, width, same seed = same sequence, fixed_take.

### Outing content: karaoke songs, escape clue recipes, dinner tastes (roadmap R3, R4, R6; `Outings`, `fx.gd`, tests `karaoke_songs`, `content`)
- **Karaoke** (`Outings.SONGS`, patchable): four invented songs (`wobble` Wobble Wobble Pop, `dirt` Dirt Road Friday, `neon` Neon Gym Class, `rap` Fast Rap Hot Mess) with `len`, `gap` (normal tier; the tier scales it by `Difficulty karaoke.gap / 0.75`), `rhythm` (gap multipliers), lane `pattern` (mix, sweep, sync, burst), `doubles` chance per tier (two arrows on one beat; hard and the rap), `win` (x the hit windows), `cuts` (share of points for 3 / 2 / 1 stars), `speed` (px/s), `unlock` and `lines` (the lyrics, 21 letters at most). `kara_chart(song, tier, rng)` builds the notes from `Difficulty.rng("karaoke")`; `_kara_setup(id)` stores `g.song / len / gap / span / speed / cuts`, and `_kara_tick`, `_kara_end`, `_paint_karaoke` and the `lyrics` fx read those, not constants (the lyrics follow the song position and show its title). With `use_rooms` ENTER on the intro opens the song card (`ask_song`, `Cards`: default first, the rap greyed out until any song has 3 stars); without rooms the default plays. `song_best` is saved. **Crowd meter** `g.mood` 0-100 (a bar beside the lanes): perfects +3 and a streak bonus, good +1.5, miss -6, wrong arrow -2; empty = the song ends early with a laugh (stars from the points so far), full = the room sings the last chorus (+1 star if you have 50%).
- **Escape room**: `make_clue(zone, recipe, digit, rng)` for four zones (`CLUE_ZONES`: board, clock, plaque, shelf), recipes in `RECIPES` (sum / product / missing digit; hour / twice the hour; number / roman numeral / letter; book volume / bookmark ribbons); `clue_answer(clue)` solves a clue from what it shows and tests compare it with the code. `Difficulty escape.digits` (3, 3, 4) sets the code length everywhere (keypad, hud, overlay bulls and cows); the 4th clue is the **bookshelf** (`clue_shelf` zone at set x 52, 36 px wide, clear of the NPC spots; on a 3 digit game it is a red herring from `HERRINGS`). `escape.dial` (1, 1, 0): on easy and normal a wrong code also tells you bulls and cows (the second route, still costs the penalty); hard is silent. Hints: `FRIEND_HINTS[friend][zone]` in the voice of the first friendly cast member (Alan the chalkboard, Dan the wall clock, Garrison the lock / keypad, Maya the plaque add a `RECIPE_TIPS` line). 3 stars at 70 s left (55 s on a 4 digit code). The fx draw `g.clues[i].show`.
- **Dinner**: 20 `TOPICS` with tags (light, deep, touchy; family topics in `PROTECTED_TOPICS` are never dreaded); each round `_dinner_deal` picks 4 you have not used with at least one safe (nobody dreads it) and one trap. `PEOPLE` has 3 loves and 1-2 dislikes per person; a new save swaps one or two (`taste(who)`, rolled from `Difficulty.rng_for("tastes")`, saved in `Outings.to_dict` as `tastes`, `version` 2); a save without `version` gets `classic_taste` (the old fixed tastes) so what it knew stays true. **Mood of the day** (`DINNER_MOODS`, rolled per diner at the start, shown as faces in the HUD strip, the caption and the overlay): +1 / 0 / -1 on every answer (`diner_points`, 0-3 per diner); the reaction face still shows the taste. **Owner pays**: owner campaign with rooms, ENTER on the intro opens the budget card (`BUDGETS`: cheap $150 mood -1, normal $300, fancy $520 mood +1; `set_budget` settles the difference with the $300 charged on arrival).

### Play-through fixes (days 1-5 of the tech campaign; test `tests/bugfix.gd`)
- **Complaints** skip closed tickets (text, delivery and the answer). **PSA CLOSE** on an unfixed ticket asks first (`Computer.close_ask`: CLOSE ANYWAY / KEEP WORKING, Esc cancels; CLOSE now sits at the far end, away from NOTE); reopens cost coworkers at most -3 opinion a day (`Social.MAX_REOPEN_LOSS`).
- **HUD** is hidden while a Card is open. An **RMM fix keeps running when the computer is closed** (playbook / install jobs are dropped; leaving the RMM session shows JOB CANCELLED). The RMM list dot is red for any endpoint with a problem or an open unfixed ticket.
- **Onsite**: `create_job` adds the round trip (2 x drive + 30 min) to the ticket's SLA (the clock is paused on the road); the drive warning's at-risk count skips the ticket you are about to work; mishaps only fire when their `object` fits the job (`Onsite.event_fits`: a PC event never interrupts a printer job).
- **Sales**: a client signed today sends no tickets until tomorrow (`Sales.signed_day`, `takes_tickets`), matching Vic's mail.
- **Commute** ends with a toast (`Shift._arrival_text`: N MIN LATE, K TICKETS WAITING / EARLY / ON TIME). **Mail**: Enter works from the first press, moving the selection (wide layout) or opening marks mail read, the list scrolls and is wider, long text clips with `..`.
- **Timed story events** (vignettes) fire when `World.clock` passes `at` and the clock is running; they can show up late only while the clock is paused or jumped (morning cards, an onsite trip moves the clock 1.5+ hours) or when the Cards queue is busy. This is intended: Heartbleed at 10:30 appears on return from a trip.
- **Fix failure rate**: one roll per finished fix (`Computer._finish_job`): `Skills.FAIL[ps_level]` (25/18/10/4/0/0 %) + `Shift.fail_add()` (up to +18% under 45 energy, -3% rested) + `Growth.fail_adjust`; wrong fixes and retries roll too. No double roll, so 25-45% is normal for a tired level 0-1 tech.
