### Hot dogs and the lunch break (roadmap R29; `grill_game.gd`, `outings.gd`, `career.gd`, `resume.gd`, `poses.lua`; tests `tests/grill.gd` (`_dogs`), `tests/lunch_break.gd`, `tests/gestures.gd`)
- **Hot dogs** (`GrillGame`, `params.dogs` = the share of order items that are dogs; `Difficulty` `grill.dogs` [0.3, 0.35, 0.4], 0 = a burger-only cookout, the default of the class): a grate slot has a `kind`, `burger` or `dog`. A dog cooks 1.3x as fast (`DOG_RATE`), reads **raw** (a side under `DOG_RAW` 0.3), **dog** (done: both sides from 0.3, none over `DOG_BURN` 0.85) or **burnt**, and needs ONE flip: the window is `DOG_WIN_LO` 0.25 to 0.25 + 0.45 x `grill.window`, flipped too soon the side keeps 70% (no tear), too late it chars like a burger. Orders mix items ("1 WELL, 1 DOG", `need` / `have` have a `dog` key; code that builds tickets by hand may leave it out, every read uses `.get`); a done dog serves only a dog line, a raw or burnt dog is tossed. A fresh slot is a dog when the open orders want more dogs than the grate holds (`_next_kind`, no random draw; the first `round(4 x share)` slots start as dogs). Stars, tickets, flare-ups and the cozy rules are unchanged (a cozy burnt dog is crispy and serves). `dogs_plated` counts them. HUD: a long thin pink-to-brown dog in its cell, letter D, order codes `1W 1D` (compact `1W1M1D` with three kinds), the ticket patience bar moved to x + 54.
- **Career**: counters `dogs_served` (HOT DOGS SERVED) and `lunches` (LUNCH BREAKS); medals **LUNCH BUNCH** (5 lunch breaks) and **DOG DAYS** (20 hot dogs) (in `strings.txt` as `Career.MEDALS.39` / `.40`). **Warehouse stars (Nov 2026)**: `Career.GAMES` (FULL HOUSE, derived `stars3`, 3 stars in all four classic games) is unchanged. `Career.WAREHOUSE_GAMES` = cornhole, grill, chair; `note_outing` already stores best stars as `stars.<game>` for every game (so no new save keys; an old save simply has 0), the derived `wh_stars3` counts warehouse games at 3 stars, and the medal **WAREHOUSE ALL-STAR** (`wh_stars3 >= 3`, `Career.MEDALS.42`, desc "3 STARS IN EACH WAREHOUSE GAME") needs all three. The career screen OUTINGS page gained CHAIR RACE BEST beside CORNHOLE BEST and COOKOUT BEST. Lunch-break cookouts also feed `stars.grill`. Tests in `tests/career.gd`.
- **Lunch break** (`Outings.maybe_lunch`, polled with `maybe_invite` from `_process`): in a campaign (tech, service and owner, NOT sales) on `LUNCH_TECH` [3, 7, 12, 17] (owner weeks `LUNCH_OWNER` [2, 7, 12, 17]; never an evening outing day; **Cozy Easy offers it on every day that has no evening outing**), when the clock is between 12:00 and 13:30 and nothing else has the player (no card, outing, onsite visit, trip, overtime, playbook, carried cable, stopped clock; `lunch_due()`), a card from Garrison (Alan in the owner campaign) offers **COOKOUT: BURGERS AND DOGS**, **CORNHOLE** or **SKIP LUNCH** (skipping costs nothing, `lunch_skips`). The card is shown once a day (`_lunch_offered`, reset by `Campaign.day_started`, **saved in the resume point** next to `_offered`; `lunches` and `lunch_skips` are in `Outings.to_dict`, older saves read 0). Taking it calls `start(id, false, {party, "lunch": true})` (the invited party, the warehouse trip like any outing) and marks every cast member `lunched` so nobody walks off to eat again. The lunch game is shorter: the cookout has 2 tickets fewer (min 4) and 80% of the time, cornhole plays to at most 11. **Owner: free** (no cash taken, no refund on Esc). **Rewards are small**: opinions +1 (+2 at three stars) instead of 1 + stars, energy 2 + stars (an evening out gives 4 + 2 x stars), owner burnout -(2 + stars), no reputation at three stars, a lunch counts as `lunches` and NOT as an `outings` count (PEOPLE PERSON); leaving at the intro just walks back to the desk without the usual goodwill loss. `close()` then calls `_lunch_after()`: the clock moves on `LUNCH_LEN` 0.75 h (never past 16:45) and every open ticket loses 0.75 h x half the onsite `SLA_PER_HOUR` of SLA (floor 35% of its total; **nothing ages on Cozy Easy**), with a toast. `opts.lunch` is cleared on close. Free Play is unchanged: E on a board or the grill still opens the plain outing.
- Tests: `tests/lunch_break.gd`, `tests/grill.gd`, `tests/gestures.gd`.

### Darts and the warehouse's odds and ends (roadmap R38; `darts_game.gd`, `outings.gd`, `fx.gd`, `career.gd`, `difficulty.gd`; tests `tests/darts.gd`, `tests/warehouse_play.gd`, `tests/warehouse_play_shots.gd`)
- **Darts** is a CASUAL game, not an evening outing: `Outings.GAMES.darts` has `casual = true` and cost 0 (`cost_range()` and `pick_game()` skip casual entries, so the invitation card and the Free Play O list are unchanged), `Outings.start("darts")` always sets `opts.casual` (the toolbox GAMES list has DARTS too, `fixed_take` = the first-play numbers). **E at the dart board** (`fx.gd` kind `dart_board`, set x 1047, low priority, still never steals E) calls `Outings.offer_darts()`: a card "Darts" (LET'S PLAY / NEVER MIND) naming the coworkers who are up for it (`darts_party()`: one or two from the roster, a seeded pick from its own stream `rng_for("darts_party", ...)`, no hostile ones; nobody = "you against the house"), then `start("darts", false, {party, casual: true})` takes you to the warehouse like any in-room game (spawn `from_outing`, everyone not in the party steps out and drifts back at the end, you return to where you stood). `darts_ready()` is false (and the board just makes one of its old jokes, `DART_LINES`) while a card, an outing, a visit, overtime, a playbook, a carried cable, a stopped clock or the last quarter hour of the campaign day is in the way. Esc leaves early: "YOU PUT THE DARTS BACK. NOBODY MINDS.", no stars, no goodwill loss, no time spent.
- **Rules** (`DartsGame`, a `RefCounted` with no autoload references; `params` come from `Outings._darts_params`, the seeded streams `Difficulty.rng("darts")` and `rng("darts_zone")`): you and 1 to 3 opponents (`ORDER` Alan, Garrison, Dan, Maya, John; `DARTS_MAX` 3 with a party, 2 without; nobody = THE HOUSE) throw **3 darts a turn for 3 rounds** (turn order You, then the crew, `READY` 1.0 s, a crew windup 0.6 s, flight 0.35 s, `PULL` 1.9 s while the three darts sit in the board and the turn total shows), highest total wins (a perfect set is 540). The board is the real one in millimetres (`score_at`): bull r 6.35 = 50, outer bull r 15.9 = 25, treble ring 99 to 107 mm = number x 3, double ring 162 to 170 mm = number x 2, anything else on the board is the wedge number, beyond 170 mm nothing; wedges `SEGMENTS` 20 1 18 4 13 6 10 15 2 17 3 19 7 16 8 11 14 9 12 5 clockwise from the top. **The aim reticle** drifts over the board on a Lissajous loop `reticle_at(t) = zc + (sin(w t + zp), 0.85 sin(1.31 w t + 1.7 zp + 1)) x drift x steadiness(t)`: the wobble settles to `STEADY_MIN` 0.45 of its size at `STEADY_T` 1.3 s and your arm tires back to full by 2.6 s (so there is a moment to wait for), **E / Space / Enter / pad X or A throws at wherever it is** and the dart lands there plus gaussian scatter `darts.noise`. **Moving sweet spots**: every dart `roll_zone()` draws the loop's centre (+-22 mm), speed factor (0.88 to 1.14) and phase from the zone stream (`Difficulty.rng("darts_zone")`, the same stream `Difficulty.zone("darts", lo, hi)` reads), so the best moment moves from dart to dart; trebles are only in reach of the wide wobble at the start or the tired end, the bull and the high singles of the steady moment. **The crew** throw at a target (the treble 20 for skill 0.55 and up, the bull for the rest) with a gaussian error lerp(40, 12, skill) mm: `TEMPERS` Alan 0.60, Garrison 0.50 streaky (+0.12 x hot after a 40+ dart, minus after a miss), Dan 0.44 wild (14% of darts with 2.6x the error), Maya 0.56, John 0.48 show-off (+0.12 with two opponents or after your 40+ dart, 18% trick darts with twice the error); `opp_skill` (the tier and the mastery) is added. **Stars**: win by `BIG_WIN` 40 or more = 3, win = 2, a tie or a loss by less than `CLOSE` 25 = 1, else 0 (1 at least on Cozy Easy, `floor`). Bot results (human timing 0.05 s): the first-play numbers win about 18 / 15 / 13 of 20 against Dan and John on easy / normal / hard, pressing at a random moment loses 17 / 17 / 19 of 20.
- **Tiers and the ramp** (`Difficulty.TABLE`, [easy, normal, hard]): `darts.drift` [46, 58, 70] mm (the loop's size), `darts.speed` [1.5, 1.9, 2.4] rad/s, `darts.noise` [6, 7, 8] mm (fixed), `darts.opp_skill` [-0.08, 0, 0.06]. `MASTERY_GAMES` has `darts`, `MASTERY_KEYS` the drift, the speed and the crew's skill (not the scatter), so a good play (2+ stars, `Career.note_outing` -> `good.darts`) tightens the loop like every other game, and `_mastery_lines` shows `MASTERY n/10` on the result page.
- **In the room** (`Outings` constants `DART_X` 992 = the throw line, `DART_BOARD_X` 1046, `DART_SPOTS` 968, 950, 932, 914 = where the others wait, `DARTS_LIFT` 166, `DARTS_PAN` 10): `_darts_seat()` puts the thrower of the turn at the line and everybody else to the left of it facing the board (the `toss` pose throws), the HUD strip (164 px) shows `R2/3`, every total (the thrower bright) and three pips for the darts left; above its right end a **zoomed board** (`_draw_dart_zoom`, 44 px, the real rings, this turn's darts coloured by thrower, and on your aim the reticle with a ring that is smaller when the wobble is steady). One hint line: `E = THROW  ESC = LEAVE` (`WAIT FOR YOUR THROW` otherwise). The world board is the 21 px baked one in the `dart_panel` sprite: `fx` kind `dart_board` (z_index 1 so it draws over the panel sprite) sticks a coloured 2 px dart with a steel tip at the scaled hit point (10.4 px for 170 mm) and flies the dart in from the throw line in an arc; the panel's baked darts stay. Cheers and groans reuse `CREW_LINES` kinds `hole` (a 40+ dart or the bull, said by somebody else) and `miss` (a dart off the board), with `!` / `?` / heart bubbles; sounds `dart_hit`, `bag_hole` (bull), `ui_tick` (the darts come out). No new sounds, no new set art, `warehouse_set.lua` untouched.
- **Rewards** (`Outings._darts_finish`, `_casual_after`): Free Play scores nothing. In a campaign a round costs `DARTS_LEN` 0.25 game hours (the clock moves on, the open queue ages at half the onsite rate, nothing on Cozy Easy, a toast says so), gives `DARTS_ENERGY` +2 energy on the first two rounds of the campaign day (tech and service; owner: burnout -2; sales: none), and on the FIRST round of a day +1 opinion with each person who played (`darts_day` / `darts_today`, saved in `Outings.to_dict` with `darts_plays`, older saves read 0). Nothing else: no reputation, not an outing (`outings` and PEOPLE PERSON do not count it), free for the owner.
- **Career**: counters `bulls` (BULLSEYES), `darts_best` (best total), `casual_games`, `stars.darts` (DARTS BEST on the OUTINGS page) and `good.darts`; medal **BULLSEYE** (43rd, `bulls >= 1`, "HIT THE BULL AT DARTS", `Career.MEDALS.43` in `strings.txt`, appended at the end of the board). `Career.WAREHOUSE_GAMES` stays cornhole, grill and chair: WAREHOUSE ALL-STAR still means 3 stars in each of THOSE three, darts is a casual extra with its own medal.
