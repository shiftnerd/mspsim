### Service manager campaign (Dan): the crew as people and the Dispatch board (C5 / V2; `crew.gd`, `dispatch.gd`, `service.gd`, `world.gd`; tests `tests/crew.gd`, `tests/dispatch.gd`, `tests/dispatch_shots.gd`)
- **Real NPC techs**: `World.CREW_CAST` (Sam = `office_intern` sheet, main floor desk 1 `Seat1`; Terry = `office_adult` sheet, **hot-desks at the conference table**, `Seat6`, because every other desk is taken) next to `CAST` (untouched, still six). `World.row(name)` returns the row of either. `roster_gone()` removes them unless the mode is service and `Crew.has(n)` and not `is_gone(n)`; `apply_roster()` brings them in (a tech who quit disappears next morning). They walk, take coffee, copier, bathroom, lunch (at their desk) and meetings like everybody; busy techs stay at the desk, idle ones wander, a tech with a networking ticket may go to the closet (`Crew.plan_kinds` used by `World._plan`). A stuck or overloaded tech shows a `?` / `!` bubble (`npc.flag_glyph`, set by `Service._flag_nodes` twice a second); a stuck tech that calls an owner walks to Alan's or Garrison's office (`plan_for` kinds `owner_alan` / `owner_garrison`). Outing spots: they stand at Dan's spot. `npc.DESKS` has Sam = desk 1; Talk has voices for Sam and Terry.
- **Crew model** (`Crew`): per tech `skill` (speed, as before), five skill **areas** (`areas`: powershell, networking, servers, security, people, floats; level = floor), `focus` (an area he trains 0.02 a day and fits better), morale, stress, retain, mentored, `over_t` / `over_days`, `done`, `stalls`. Tickets have an area and a **need** (`FIX_AREA`, `FIX_NEED`, P1 +1; tag e.g. `SEC2`). `gap_of` = need - level (1 or more = above his head), `match_mult` (fit 1.0-1.12, a gap of 1 = 0.6x, 2 = 0.2x), `reopen_add` (-3% for a clear fit, +12% a gap level), `fit` (match x load x mood + focus, shown as FIT %), `best_for` (autopilot), `status` IDLE / WORKING / OVERLOAD (3+ tickets) / STUCK / GONE, `rate`, `load_hours`, `utilisation` (queued hours over hours left today). Doing tickets teaches a little (more when it stretched him); `skill_gain` (1:1s, mentoring) trains his focus or weakest area. End of day: a quarter of the day with 3+ tickets costs 2 morale, adds stress and an `over_days` point; 3 bad days in a row with morale under 40 add quit odds; a quitter's tickets go back on the board (`Service.release_all`) and he owes you a **farewell card** next morning (`Service._farewells`, `CREW_TEXT.farewell`). Old (C4) saves load: numbers kept, areas and focus start from the roster, Sam and Terry become NPCs.
- **Work and assignments** (`Service`): `Owner._work_queue` calls `Service.work_queue(seconds)` in this mode (tech and owner are untouched). The crew's total (`Owner.team_speed`) is split by speed; each tech works only the tickets on his desk (priority, then SLA), idle techs help the busy ones at half rate (`ASSIST`), unassigned tickets are not worked (an unassigned P1 / P2 ages 50% faster, and after 90 s Garrison notices: -1 trust). Working a ticket above his level for 16 s makes him **STUCK** (text to you, toast, bubble, no work from him); you can reassign it (frees him, +2 morale, 6 s handover cost), close it yourself (+4) or take it; after 20 s (unless the policy is OFF) he **calls an owner** (the ticket moves to the escalation lane, trust -2) and after 50 s he pushes through alone (slow, +15% reopen odds, -2 morale). `Service.assign(id, who, by_hand)` is the one entry point (lanes: `""` unassigned, a tech, `Dan`, `ESC` = Alan for servers / networking / PowerShell, Garrison for security / people; each owner takes 2 a day at `OWNER_RATE` 1.6, trust -2 each; counted in `dispatched` / `escalated` and `Career` counter `dispatched`, a row in the career screen). `Service.auto` (saved): `all` (the crew picks up by fit, default and what V1 did), `low` (P3 / P4 only), `off` (you assign everything). The day scorecard TEAM group has a DISPATCHED row.
- **ConnectWhy Dispatch** (`scripts/dispatch.gd`, an object held by Computer as `Computer.dispatch`; desktop icon and start menu entry in service mode; window `_paint_dispatch`): tabs BOARD, CAPACITY, SCORE and an AUTO button. BOARD: lanes UNASSIGNED, each tech, DAN (YOU), OWNERS; chips show id, skill tag + need, client, SLA text and a bar, a priority stripe, `?` when stuck; a lane head shows a `!` / `?` state or the count. **Keys** (`Dispatch.key(name)`, the test entry point; `Computer._dispatch_key` maps the real keys): Tab / Shift+Tab walk the chips, Left / Right pick the lane, Up / Down the chip, **Enter picks up, Left / Right choose the target, Enter drops, Esc cancels**; A auto policy, T take it, E escalate, U unassign, C / B / S tabs, F or Enter in CAPACITY cycles the focus. **Mouse**: press a chip and drag to a lane (`mouse_down/move/up`), click a lane head or a tab; while carrying a click on a lane drops. While carrying, the footer shows FIT % and a warning (`NEEDS SEC2 HE HAS 1`, `HE IS BUSY`). CAPACITY: per tech queued-hours bar against hours left (tick = 100%), utilisation %, morale pips, state, focus tag and a red `!` on overload / stuck, owners' use of the day, crew totals. SCORE: today so far (`Service.today`) and the daily scores of the last ten days as a line with the weekly (5-day) averages. **Fuzz** (`Dispatch.fuzz` -1 = from `Build.level("dispatch") < 3`, 0 exact, 1 fuzzy): SLA to the minute (`*3M`), the bar in quarters, fit as a word, utilisation in quarters. **Narrow (v, under 280 px)**: one lane at a time with a lane button strip, `<` `>` and Left / Right; capacity stacks two lines per tech. 368x128, 320x180 and 180x320 were checked with `tests/dispatch_shots.gd` (renderer, `-- --format=wide|h|v`, `user://dispatch_*.png`, 3x upscaled).
- Patchable text: `Service.CREW_TEXT` (stuck texts, neglect, farewell card; `Strings.ROOTS` has it). Tests: `tests/crew.gd` (cast, areas, needs, focus, plan kinds, other modes unchanged) and `tests/dispatch.gd` (fit, stuck, overload, quit, escalation, capacity numbers, board key flow, drag, fuzz, narrow lane switching, save / continue, an old save, a 20-day run through the real loop with the board in use).

### Sales campaign (John): the deep loop (C2, phase S2; tests `tests/deals.gd`, `quote_builder.gd`, `handover.gd`, `churn.gd`, `deals_shots.gd`; scripts `deals.gd`, `quote_builder.gd`, `handover.gd`, `retention.gd`: static `RefCounted` helpers preloaded by `SalesCampaign`, which keeps the state)
- **Deals are records** (`Deals`): a lead dictionary gains `touch` (last touch week), `notes`, `follow`, `quote`, `margin`, `discount`, `promises`, `quoted_day`. Stages LEAD / MEETING / QUOTED / WON / LOST. Calls, visits, quotes, CHASE (key F, an hour) and accepting a lead touch the deal. **Decay**: untouched 2 weeks = quality -3 a week, `COLD_WEEKS` (4) untouched = WENT COLD. `Deals.risk(l)` is the 0-100 risk meter (going cold / ByteBrothers steal chance / the churn meter of a won client). A quote that does not win on the spot (the same single `Difficulty.rng("deals")` draw as before decides the win) is **held** 45% of the time: QUOTED with `quoted_day`; each Monday (`Deals.resolve_pending`, seeded `rng_for("rival", id*100+day)`) ByteBrothers steals it with `0.08 + 0.09 x weeks untouched + 0.04 x weeks quoted - 0.10 x follow-ups` (x 0.8 / 1 / 1.2 by tier, clamped 4-60%), else the client may say yes (`quote.win x 0.8 + 0.12 x follow-ups`) or after 3 weeks goes quiet.
- **CRM**: INFO (key I, `crm_page`) flips the detail pane to the deal record: risk bar, touches, the promise list tagged K / R / D (kept / renegotiated / dropped, "+n more" when it does not fit), the last note; for a won deal gap, mood, kept / broken counts and the 90-day week. Buttons CALL VISIT QUOTE CHASE DROP (UPSELL on a won deal, key U) INFO. `crm_view` "list" | "build" swaps the list for the builder (Esc / `Computer.back()` returns to the list: hooked in `computer.gd` `escape()` / `back()`).
- **Quote builder** (`QuoteBuilder`, state `SalesCampaign.qb`): fields METHOD, PRICE (list / +10%), DISCOUNT (0 / 10 / 20), SLA TIER (basic / standard / premium), scope items BACKUP / UPDATES+AV / SECURITY (`Deals.EXTRAS`: fee, cost, capacity points, fit with the lead's pain), ONBOARDING (1 / 2 / 4 weeks). Up / Down row, Left / Right value, S or Enter send, Tab page, B / Esc back; mouse on the `<` `>` buttons and rows. `Deals.gauges(lead, q)` holds all the maths: WIN CHANCE (the old table plus extras that fit the pain +4 points, that do not -1.5, onboarding rush +5 / slow -3, discount +0.6 per percent), MARGIN (extras blend fee and cost; Alan's floor is a tick on the bar), PROMISE GAP against `capacity()` (crew, open queue, recent deals; extras add 0.3 / 0.2 / 0.4, a rush 0.7, slow -0.3) and CASH over four weeks (4 x gross profit - onboarding $250 / $100 / $0 - discovery - commission). Inline blocks with the owner's line: ALAN under the floor, GARRISON refusing 20% for a small account (or signing it off), CLOCK (no hours left), MORGAN's soft warning at a gap of 1.5+. A blocked quote cannot be sent. Old `{method, promise, price: "d10"}` dictionaries still work through `Deals.norm`. Wide and h: fields left, gauges right; under 250 px (portrait) two pages (fields plus a one-line summary, then gauges and SEND).
- **Handover** (`Handover`): `close_deal` thanks you, then `Handover.queue`: Morgan (Seat3) and Dan (Seat4) are sent to the conference room (`World.send_to_meeting`), the objective line says HANDOVER: JOIN MORGAN AND DAN IN THE CONFERENCE ROOM, `SalesCampaign._process` polls and starts the scene when you stand in the conference room (`Meeting.in_conference()`; one at a time, more wins wait in `handover_queue`). One card per promise: KEEP (left = gap x 0.5), RENEGOTIATE (left 0, mood -3, Morgan and Dan +1), DROP (left = the gap, Dan -1 when it was 0.5+). Starting mood = 72 - 8 x gap left - 3 x renegotiated; the gap left is the service risk (churn start `10 + 12 x gap + 4 x dropped`) and a gap of 0.5+ still queues C1's complaint card. Dan and Morgan show bubbles (`npc._bubble`) and Dan's closing card states mood and risk. A handover nobody attends becomes "just the paperwork" on Monday (`paperwork_all`: every promise dropped, Morgan texts). The old `SalesCampaign.handover(deal, how)` maps honest / brief / gloss to renegotiate / keep / drop all (a deal from an old save without promises keeps the old arithmetic).
- **90-day churn and clawback** (`Retention.week_tick`, from `settle`): a campaign week is 7 game days, so `Deals.WINDOW_WEEKS` 13 = the 90 days and `NEW_CLIENT_WEEKS` 4 = the first 30. The churn meter (0-100) gains 5 x unresolved gap, 5 per breached new-client ticket (`ticket.newclient`, counted once), (50 - mood) / 6 under mood 50 and 5 per week onboarding slips (go-live is due at `onboard_due`, delayed while `capacity() < 1.2`); it loses 2 above mood 70 and 1.5 per kept promise. Inside the window a client leaves at 100, or each week with chance `(meter - 50) / 150` (`rng_for("churn", ...)`). **Clawback** takes `CLAW[plan]` (salary 50%, balanced and commission 100%, stated on the pay plan card) of the deal's commission back (`commission_total`, `week_clawback` in the weekly profit and in the COMMISSION EARNED row), removes the MRR from an open quarter, reputation -1, Garrison -1, and a card names the reason (promise gap, breaching tickets, unhappy from the start, slow onboarding). After the window the deal is `safe`.
- **Renewals and upsells**: `Retention.schedule_base()` puts the five inherited clients on a renewal schedule (weeks 6-20, `base_book`, $650+ a week); won deals renew `RENEW_AFTER` (14) weeks after signing. The Monday card (`renewal_card`): renew as is / RUN A BUSINESS REVIEW (an hour, the owner campaign's QBR talks from `Onsite.MEETINGS.qbr`: numbers +6 mood, listen +10, offer a service = upsell) / raise the price 10%. `renew_chance = 0.40 + mood/250 + 0.04 x kept - 0.06 x broken` (+ review bonus, -0.15 for a rise), rolled from `rng_for("renew", ...)`; a renewal pays half the plan's commission rate on the fee, a lost one takes the fee off the books (`lost_base`) and costs reputation 1. **Upsell** (`upsell_card`, CRM key U, an hour): backup $110, updates+AV $80, security $95 a week, 70% yes at mood 60+ (else 40%), counted as new MRR for the quota (`expansion_total`) with commission.
- **New-client SLA priority** (hook in `Msp.create_problem`): a book deal inside its first 30 days with a premium (`high`) SLA that is still kept (or not yet handed over) claims about a quarter of the ticket stream per deal (max 60%, `rng_for("newticket", id)`): the ticket's priority goes one level higher and `ticket.newclient` holds the deal name. A dropped or renegotiated SLA, a standard SLA and later weeks get nothing. The book clients are not `Msp.CLIENTS`, so this models them as a share of the stream.
- **Career** rows (SALES section in `career.gd`): DEALS WON, CLAWBACKS, RENEWALS WON, UPSELLS SOLD (`Career.add("deals_won" | "clawbacks" | "renewals" | "upsells" | "qbrs")`). The review's first page adds a CLAWBACKS / RENEWALS / UPSELLS / RIVAL WINS line. All saved in the `sales_campaign` section (new keys, old saves default).
- Tests: `deals.gd`, `quote_builder.gd`, `handover.gd`, `churn.gd`, `sales_campaign.gd`, `crm.gd`, `deals_shots.gd`.
- Not done: onsite QBR for book clients (they have no site); quota and churn numbers are untuned (the 20-week test wins about 30 quotes).
